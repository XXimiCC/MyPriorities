# Quick start

> What to do in the first five minutes, and what happens after that.

## 1. Pick a set

On first launch the app shows three explanation screens and offers a
[ready-made set of priorities](/screens/presets).

![Picking a starting set](/shots/onboarding-presets.png)

Take whichever is closest. The list can be reworked in full afterwards — order,
names, colors, contents, [all of it](/screens/edit). There is no wrong choice
here.

## 2. Mark what you already did today

![The empty priorities screen](/shots/home-empty.png)

Tap “+” next to a direction. One tap is half an hour.

Do not count precisely. Mark in large chunks: two hours of work is four taps,
and that is closer to the truth than trying to remember what happened between
14:20 and 14:50.

Overshot? Tap the row itself and fix the counter.

## 3. Mark how you feel

The battery is in the top right corner.

![Picking your charge](/shots/home-battery-sheet.png)

Mark **the change**, not the fact: the app counts the time between switches, so
one tap in the morning describes the whole morning.

## 4. Come back once a day

That is all there is to it. A mark takes two touches, and by the next day the
app already knows something about you.

Forgot to mark anything? Tap “Fill in an earlier day” and add it after the
fact — the strip remembers two weeks.

## What shows up over time

| When | What |
|---|---|
| From day two | Your [streak](/guide/glossary#серия) and the per-day breakdown in the stats |
| After a week | The first [observations](/topics/insights) and a full energy chart |
| After a month | Week-to-week comparison, weekdays against weekends, concentration |
| Along the way | [Achievements](/screens/achievements) — they start with the very first block |

## What to set up once you settle in

- **[The price of a tap](/screens/settings#цена-одного-клика)**, if half an hour
  is not your unit. There are 15, 20, 25, 45 and 60 minutes too.
- **[Skills](/screens/skills)**, if there is something you put years into. Type
  in the hours you have already collected — the ladder will not start at zero.
- **[Modules](/topics/modules)**, if the extra tabs are in your way.
- **[The charge wallpaper](/topics/wallpaper)**, so your state is visible without
  opening the app.
- **[Language](/screens/settings)** — the app picks it from Telegram or your
  browser, and the switch is the first thing on the settings screen.

## What is worth doing once

**[Download a backup](/topics/backup).** Until you sign in with Telegram, the
history sits on this device only and there is nowhere to restore it from. Takes
half a minute.
