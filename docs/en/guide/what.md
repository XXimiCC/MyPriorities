# What this is

::: tip English is the entrance, not the whole building
Three pages of this manual are in English: this one, the
[quick start](/en/guide/quick-start) and the home page. Everything past them —
a page per screen, how the internals work, the development notes — is in
Russian. The app itself speaks both: see [Language](/screens/settings) in its
settings.
:::

**My Priorities** is a Telegram mini app about where your time and your own
energy actually go.

Plans lie, marks do not. The app will not tell you how to live: it shows how you
live already and lets you see the tilt with your own eyes.

![The priorities screen](/shots/home-frame-en.png)

## How it works, in two sentences

You keep a **list of directions** — work, family, health, whatever yours are.
Gave one a focused block of time? Tap “+”. One tap is half an hour of your life,
put into that direction.

Separately you mark **your charge**: full, medium, running on empty, or
recharging. That is about your own resource, not your phone battery. The app
counts the time between switches.

A week later the stats screen shows two things: **where the time went** and
**what state it went in**. Four hours of work at full charge and four hours on
empty are two different four hours.

## What it is made of

| Part | About |
|---|---|
| [Priorities](/screens/home) | Where today, this week and this month went |
| [Charge](/screens/charge) | What state you are in and how long you spend in it |
| [Skills](/screens/skills) | The things you put years into. Seventeen steps of mastery |
| [Stats](/screens/stats) | Every cut: by priority, by day, by state |
| [Achievements](/screens/achievements) | 75 marks: some unlock on their own, some you mark yourself |
| [Settings](/screens/settings) | Presets, modules, the price of a tap, data, reset |

Skills, achievements and observations [can be switched off](/topics/modules) —
the app falls back to its core.

## Three decisions that explain the rest

### The unit is a tap, not a minute

Typing exact times would turn marking into work, and work is what people stop
doing after a week. So it is “+” and nothing else.

Two things follow from that:
[taps are stored, not hours](/topics/data#почему-хранятся-клики-а-не-часы), and
changing [the price of a tap](/screens/settings#цена-одного-клика) recounts the
whole history at once.

### The bar is measured against the leader, not against the day

Whatever eats the most is always full width; the rest are drawn as fractions of
it. Percentages of a 24-hour day would show that all of life is a set of thin
slivers, and the tilt would be lost in them. And the tilt is the reason the app
exists.

### The app never advises

[Observations](/topics/insights) state a fact and stop. No “you should”, no “try
to”. What to do about it is not the app’s business.

## What it does not have

**Sign-up.** You open it and it works. No email, no password, no form: the
[history lives on your device](/topics/data), and the app knows nothing about
you until you sign in with Telegram yourself, for the sake of a second device.

**A subscription or ads.**

**Notifications or a widget.** Both need something a mini app cannot have —
see [Telegram-only](/topics/telegram-only).

**A light theme.** Dark only: neon on a light background does not read at all.
