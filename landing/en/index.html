Why bother

Run a check on your own reality

Find out what you actually spend time on, what takes your energy and what gives it back, and which skills you are really building. Learn to focus on what matters.

“A year went by, and I cannot say on what”

Plans lie, marks do not. You think you live for your family, and a week of marks shows two thirds of it went into work. That is not a reproach, it is a number you cannot argue with.

“I am tired, and I cannot remember what from”

Four hours of work at full charge and four hours on empty are two different four hours. The app asks what drained the battery, and a month later you have a list — with the same line at the top every time.

“Years went in, and there is nothing to be proud of”

Noticing the ground already covered makes the walking easier. The hours you put into a skill stop being a feeling and become a step, with a concrete number to the next one.

The plan

What the app is for

Mark where you invest your time and energy. A week later you get the picture and your first conclusions, tune your life by them, and start moving where you actually wanted to.

In 7 days you will answer four questions for yourself

What do I actually spend my time on?

Bars by direction for a day, a week and a month. The leader is always full width, so the tilt is visible without doing any math.

What takes my energy, and what gives it back?

Every time you run to empty, the app asks why. A month later the answers add up into a list — and the top line is usually the same one.

How much energy do I have through the day, and how does it move?

How many hours you lived in each state, when your battery usually runs down, and how your average charge moved from week to week.

Which skills do I have, and at what level?

A step from novice to master for every skill, the hours collected and the pace over 30 days — including the years that came before the app.

Now you can actually see yourself moving toward what you wanted.

Your own energy

Four states

How much energy do you really have through the day, and what exactly is taking it?

HIGH

Full charge

You have it in you for hard things. Take on what needs a head.

MEDIUM

Medium charge

Working order. Routine is fine, heroics are not.

LOW

Running on empty

Nothing left in the tank. Whatever gets done now gets forced.

CHARGING

Recharging

You are refilling: sleep, quiet, a walk, doing nothing.

All you mark is the moment the state changed — the app counts the time between switches itself. One tap in the morning describes the whole morning, and by evening you have the day laid out without filling in a single form.

Before you start

The short answers

What does it cost?
Nothing. No subscription, no ads, no paid features — everything described above is open from the start.
How much time does it take a day?
Seconds. A couple of taps when you gave something time, and a couple more when your state changed. There is nothing to fill in and nowhere to type.
Do I need Telegram?
No. The app opens in an ordinary browser and works there in full. Signing in with Telegram is only for seeing one history on your phone and your computer at once.
Where are my marks kept?
On your device. Until you sign in, the app knows nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere.
What if I have been at this for years already?
Type in the hours you have collected when you create the skill — the ladder starts not at zero but at the step you already reached.
What if I quit after a week?
A week of marks already answers the first question. Even if it goes no further, you will know where your time goes.
